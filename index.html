Installing on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above, then open the downloaded file.
  2. Android will ask whether to allow installing from this source — tap Settings, turn it on, then come back. You only do this once.
  3. Tap Install, then Open. The app appears on the home screen like any other.
  4. That is it — it works with no internet from then on, on a plane, in the car, anywhere.

Why that permission step? The app is not on the Play Store, so Android treats it as coming from outside. It asks for no permissions at all — not even internet — so nothing on the phone is accessible to it.

Tip for parents: the child's stars, ticks and name are saved on that device. If you want to hand the app to a second child, there is a small Reset link at the bottom of the welcome screen — you have to press and hold it to confirm, so little fingers cannot wipe it by accident.
